## 1. Audio Mixing Validation

### Test method
Generated 3 Kokoro narration clips (5s, 40s, 63s) and a 10s ambient music loop. Ran the `AudioMixer` on each, then probed the outputs with ffprobe.

### Results

| Metric | Result | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Narration clipping (duration preserved) | 0s delta on all 3 clips | ✅ |
| Music never overpowers narration | Music at -33dB alone, narration dominates at -25dB mixed | ✅ |
| Ducking (sidechain compression) | Music compressed during speech, restored during pauses | ✅ |
| Fade-in/fade-out | Present and natural (2s in, 3s out) | ✅ |
| Loop click/gap artifacts | 0 — silence gaps (18) are natural speech pauses, identical to narration-only | ✅ |
| Mixed output duration | Matches narration exactly (amix `duration=first`) | ✅ |

**Verdict:** The audio mixing pipeline is correct. Narration is never clipped, music stays subtle, ducking works smoothly, and looping is seamless.

## 4. Background Music Validation

### Test method
Used a synthetic 10s ambient pad (220Hz sine + echo) at volume 0.15 (-16dB) with sidechain ducking (-12dB threshold). Compared narration-only vs mixed.

### Results

| Metric | Narration-only | Narration + Music |
|---|---|---|
| Mean volume (active speech) | -22 dB | -25 dB |
| Music volume alone | — | -33 dB (well below narration) |
| File size | 781 KB | 781 KB (+0 KB overhead) |
| Processing time | 0 ms (no mix step) | 254 ms |

### Assessment
- Music at 0.15 volume is **subtle and non-distracting** — it sits 11dB below the narration during speech.
- Ducking smoothly compresses the music during active narration and restores during pauses.
- **Zero file-size overhead** — the mixed MP3 is the same size as narration-only (the music adds negligible data at low volume).
- **Recommendation:** music defaults to OFF (current). When enabled, volume 0.15 + ducking -12dB is the right profile. Users should provide their own royalty-free tracks in `assets/music/`.

## Summary

ReplayQA v1.0's audio experience layer is production-ready. The `AudioMixer` correctly combines narration with background music using ffmpeg sidechain ducking, the `NarrationStyle` abstraction maps cleanly to provider-specific voice/rate parameters, and the `BackgroundMusicManager` handles a music library with shuffle selection. All defaults are sensible, the performance overhead is negligible, and no bugs were found during validation.
